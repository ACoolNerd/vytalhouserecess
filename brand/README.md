# VYTAL House Brand Integration — Immersive Experience

**Official brand version:** 1.0.0  
**Canonical source:** `ACoolNerd/VYTALHouse/brand-system`  
**Official positioning line:** **Renew • Restore • Recharge**

This repository is an experimental immersive implementation. Experimentation may change motion, layout, interaction, lighting, and spatial storytelling, but it may not change the master identity.

## Required implementation
- Consume approved assets and synchronized tokens rather than generating substitutes.
- Preserve the full wordmark and monogram geometry, aspect ratio, and clear space.
- Use **Renew • Restore • Recharge** for new public-facing work.
- Do not use the deprecated line **Recovery • Contouring • Longevity** in active experiences.
- Keep motion refined and legible; do not distort, fragment, morph, or over-effect the logo.
- Test desktop, mobile, reduced-motion preferences, contrast, loading performance, and social previews.
- Apply **Rights • Disclosure • Proof** to partners, claims, and external media.

Record the official brand-system version in releases and route identity changes through the canonical brand governance process.
